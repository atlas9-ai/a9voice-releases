Two prompts to click through, once

The installer isn’t code-signed yet. It comes from our GitHub Releases, and every later update is checked against a signature by the app itself, so you only see these prompts on the first install.

1. In your browser (Chrome or Edge): Keep

The browser may say the file “isn’t commonly downloaded”. Open the downloads list, choose Keep, and if it asks again, Keep anyway.

2. In Windows: More info, then Run anyway

When you open the installer, Windows may show Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.
