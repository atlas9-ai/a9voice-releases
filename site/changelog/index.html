CHANGELOG

What changed

What changed in each version of A9 Voice, newest first. New builds reach the Dev channel first and move to Stable once they have been tried; see Updates and channels.

1.0.3 October 15, 2026

  • AssemblyAI is now available for meeting notes and the live transcript. Add a key from the AssemblyAI tile in Speech services (it was marked Soon), choose Universal-3.5 Pro or the cheaper Universal-2, and press Test. Meeting notes tell the other people on a call apart, and the Live window streams to AssemblyAI too when it writes your notes, with the cost shown and no second provider billed. Your Dictionary words go along as key terms, and Usage & cost prices each combination. See Speech models.

1.0.2 October 15, 2026

  • Mistral (Voxtral) can now be set up for speech, with speaker separation. The Mistral tile in Speech services used to stop at "Mistral listed no chat models" because Mistral's model list wasn't read right, and nothing could be saved. It now opens with voxtral-mini-latest filled in, Test sends the short test clip and shows what it heard, and in Meeting notes Mistral labels the other people on a call so naming and voice tags work as with Deepgram. Your Dictionary words go along as spelling hints, and its price is in Usage & cost. The same list fix applies to Mistral under Language models. See Speech models.
  • A service you can't test yet can still be saved. If Test fails or can't run (no key yet), Save stays available and the tile says Not tested yet: with the reason, for speech and language services alike.

1.0.1 October 14, 2026

  • The live transcript now only runs while the live window is open, so you don't pay for it when nobody's watching. Until now each side of a call streamed to Deepgram for the whole recording at the live rate, and was then uploaded again after the call. Now it streams while the Live window is showing, plus a minute after you close it, and where it was closed the transcript says so. Your notes are made from the whole recording either way, and names you give still land on the right people. Everyone gets this new default; Settings › Notes › Live window › Live transcript has While the live window is open, Always (the old behavior) and Off. See Live window.
  • It never bills a second provider on its own. When another engine writes your notes, the live transcript uses a speech model on this PC if one is installed (free, and the window says so), and otherwise streams to Deepgram only if you choose Use Deepgram for the live transcript. With neither, the window says what it needs.
  • Usage & cost shows it. Only the seconds really sent are counted, the monthly figures use your real live hours, and Savings says when Always ran with the window closed, with a one-click Switch to While open. See Usage and cost.

1.0.0 October 14, 2026

  • Usage & cost shows what you really spend, and whether it is worth it. Every upload is written down as it happens (a meeting's live stream and its after-the-call transcript are priced separately), prices are per model, and the page has an Overview with a monthly budget and a cost-per-meeting-hour target, a History that stays short, and Prices you can edit. The new Savings tab says, for Meeting notes, Summaries & Ask and Dictation, whether you are on a good fit or could save, shows the options on a cost-versus-speed map, and can Compare on a meeting: your own audio through a second provider, side by side, with the cost shown first. Switch moves Meeting notes only. See Usage and cost.
  • A new install costs nothing to start. Setup downloads Parakeet for dictation and meeting notes, and a free Groq key covers summaries and Ask, with long meetings written in parts so they work on any model. Add custom API asks "Which service?" for DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, Together, Fireworks, Cerebras, Ollama and LM Studio, for language models and for speech.
  • Meetings you can trust. Not part of the meeting leaves lines out for good, Regenerate transcript keeps your names, a short recording-consent notice starts Notes, and the Live window holds still while you name someone and takes a yes or no on a guess in one click.
  • Works where networks get in the way. A9 Voice reaches its servers through a mirror when a work network blocks a9voice.com, and the live transcript connects on networks that inspect traffic.
  • Smaller things. Home keeps counting and has a range switch, a larger Windows Text size and Settings › General › Interface size are respected, and the Notes trial, license and update checks work the same on 1.0.0.

0.7.25 October 14, 2026

  • Usage & cost is rebuilt, and the estimate is no longer low. A meeting with the live window on is billed twice by Deepgram (streamed live, then uploaded after the call) and the old table counted only the second. Each side of a call is now written down as it is sent, live or after the call, and priced at the rate for that mode. Overview has a gauge against an optional monthly budget, the cost per meeting hour against an optional target, daily spend by provider, and your most expensive notes. See Usage and cost.
  • Prices are per model, with two speech rates. After the call and Live per audio hour, Input and Output per 1M tokens, so Claude Haiku 5.5 and Haiku 4.5 are no longer priced alike. A price you type is marked Edited and stays through updates; Reset to defaults puts the shipped ones back. Where OpenRouter reports what a call cost, that number is used.
  • History is a short list again. Today and Yesterday are open, the rest of the month is one group, every past month is one line, and a day's dictations are one row. Filter by Meetings, Dictation or Ask, search, Export CSV, and see the month on a calendar. A note nothing was recorded for says Not tracked instead of $0.00.
  • Ask and Enhance are counted now, and a note's summary uses the tokens the provider counted.

0.7.24 October 14, 2026

  • "Don't have one? Get a DeepSeek key →" under every preset's API key box (with the service's own name). DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, Together, Fireworks and Cerebras (language models) and Together and Mistral (speech) open the service's own key page in your browser. Servers on this PC and Other have no link, and a built-in service's window keeps the link under the key box after Manage key.
  • The Mistral tile in Speech services is live. It opens the Mistral (Voxtral) preset instead of sitting dimmed as Soon; once you save it, your entry is the tile. AssemblyAI, Speechmatics and Gladia stay Soon.
  • A line under each "Which service?" list says where the other kind of service is added: summaries and Ask under Language models, dictation and meeting transcripts under Speech services.

0.7.23 October 14, 2026

  • Speech services › Add custom API asks "Which service?" too. Together (Whisper and others), Mistral (Voxtral) and Speaches on this PC (no key) fill in their own address; Other (OpenAI-compatible) keeps the plain form, and entries you made before open that way, unchanged. Model is a list asked of the server (a text box with the reason when it can't), the extra header and an editable Base URL sit under Advanced, and Test sends the 2-second clip and says in plain words what's wrong. Each says it doesn't tell speakers apart, so Meeting notes show One speaker for everyone else. See Adding another service.
  • The "Add custom API" tile is one tile tall in both Speech services and Language models, however tall the tiles beside it are.

0.7.22 October 14, 2026

  • A new install now costs nothing to use. Setup downloads Parakeet for dictation and meeting notes (speakers told apart, nothing leaves your PC): V2 when Windows is displayed in English, V3 otherwise, with Use the other one. The last step is now Summaries: Summaries and Ask need a free Groq key. It takes 2 minutes and no card. A Groq key saved anywhere in setup sets Summaries, Ask and Enhance to GPT-OSS 20B on Groq, with long meetings written in parts. See Free setup. If you were using A9 Voice before this, one card on Settings › Models, Free setup available, shows what it would change and applies only what you have not chosen yourself.
  • Honest about meetings on this PC. Parakeet is the free default for dictation and works for meeting notes, but its live transcript lags and it is less reliable at telling callers apart on busy calls. Setup says so and offers Add a Deepgram key / Add an ElevenLabs key; saving one moves Meeting notes to it with speakers told apart. Settings › Models shows a quiet line and Upgrade on a local meeting engine, the engine list has a speed hint per engine, and the first note made on this PC carries a one-time tip.
  • No key yet? The note waits, and a used-up day says when it's back. A note recorded with no language model says Add a free Groq key to get a summary; add the key and every such note is summarized, oldest first, with nothing to click. When a model's allowance for the day is used up, the note shows the reset time, for example resets in 3 h (around 6:40 PM), and offers Use another model.

0.7.21 October 14, 2026

  • Add custom API asks "Which service?" first. DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, Together, Fireworks and Cerebras fill in their own address, so you paste a key and you're done. Ollama and LM Studio (on this PC) need no key. Other (OpenAI-compatible) is the old form, and entries you made before open that way, unchanged. See Adding another service.
  • Model is a list, and Test says what's wrong. Once your key is in (at once for Ollama and LM Studio), the window asks the service which chat models it has and preselects a small, cheap one; if it can't, Model stays a box and one line says why. The extra header and an editable Base URL moved under Advanced. Test names the problem in plain words: wrong key, wrong address, no such model, or Local server not running.
  • A line about your data where the terms say something. Google's free tier, DeepSeek and Mistral's free plan show one neutral sentence on the window and the tile. Nothing is shown where a service's terms say it doesn't train on what you send.

0.7.20 October 13, 2026

  • A very long meeting no longer stops at "wouldn't fit". When putting the notes of many parts together, a merge that came back cut off could leave the notes too big for the last step and the summary failed. The merge now gets more room, and if it still isn't usable the notes are put together and trimmed without it, keeping every action item and decision.

0.7.19 October 13, 2026

  • Long meetings keep every action item and decision. Checked on a real Groq key, a summary written in parts sometimes lost a task a part had found, or ran out of room on a part and had to cut it again. The parts now list every commitment and decision first and get more room when one has to be cut, and anything a part found that the finished summary left out is put back.

0.7.18 October 13, 2026

  • Long meetings now summarize on any model, including a free Groq key. A meeting too long for the Summary model's limit (Groq's free tier allows 8,000 tokens a minute, less than a 30-minute meeting) is read in parts and put together into the same summary, title, tags and action items. While it works the note says Writing summary… part 2 of 4, and waiting for Groq's per-minute limit when it waits. See Long meetings.
  • Summaries that failed as "too large" write themselves again. The first time you open this version, a note whose summary failed with request too large in the last 30 days is summarized again, oldest first, with nothing to click. A limit that stops it now says which one and when it resets, for example Groq's daily token limit for this model is used up; resets in 3 h.
  • Ask AI on a long note answers. When the whole transcript won't fit the model, it is given the summary and the passages that match the question, and the footer says answered from the summary + 14 relevant passages.
  • A new choice, Settings › Transcript & summary › Long meetings: Chunk on the same model (default) or Use fallback model. With Groq as your only language model, Settings › Models says long meetings are handled in parts.

0.7.17 October 13, 2026

  • Add someone to the meeting before they speak. + Person at the end of the Live window's strip adds a person with a dashed pill and no voice yet. It names no line and teaches no voice; it is a name you can pick from any line's menu. The × on the pill takes it away.
  • Number keys in a line's menu. The people on the call, then the people you added, are numbered 1 to 9 at the top of every line menu. Click a name, press the number, done: "this isn't Ana, it's Riley" in two keystrokes. The note page's line menu numbers its speakers the same way.
  • More lines that sound like the same person (note page, with Voices on). After you move some lines of a wrongly split speaker to another person, the note says 12 more lines from Ana Example sound like Ben Sample and lets you review them with play and ✓ / ✗. Nothing moves without a ✓, and nothing is learned or saved from the check.

0.7.16 October 13, 2026

  • Works on a work network that blocks a9voice.com. Activating a license, the Notes trial, the update check and the opt-in counts now try a9voice.atlas9.ai, a copy of the same server, when a9voice.com can't be reached or is answered by a filter's block page. A9 Voice remembers which one answered and tries it first next time. A real "no" from the server (a wrong key, a PC limit) is shown as it is and never retried elsewhere. If neither can be reached the message names both.
  • The Live transcript connects on networks that inspect traffic. Its connection used a built-in list of trusted certificate authorities, so on a network that re-signs HTTPS with the company's own certificate it failed while everything else worked, and the Live window stayed empty with no explanation. It now trusts the same Windows certificate store as the rest of A9 Voice.
  • The Live window says when it can't connect. After about 10 seconds without a connection it shows "Live transcript can't connect from this network. Recording continues; the full transcript with speakers is made when you stop." A9 Voice keeps trying for the whole recording and the line goes away when it connects. If a speech model is installed on this PC, the Live transcript switches to it and says so.

0.7.15 October 13, 2026

  • A short recording-consent notice when Notes starts. Under Try Notes free, and under Start trial on the "Want notes for this call?" card, a line says that by starting Notes you agree to get any consent the law requires before you record other people, and that some places, including several US states, require everyone's consent. Learn more opens Recording consent. Pressing the button is the agreement; there is no checkbox.
  • If your Notes trial started before you saw that, you see it once. A small card on the Notes page and the first time the recording bar appears has the same line with Got it. It never blocks, delays or stops a recording. Settings › Notes › Recording always shows the line.
  • What is kept. A9 Voice remembers on your PC when you accepted and which version of the notice you saw (never in exports). The Notes trial start and the next trial or license check send a9voice.com one number, the notice's version, and a9voice.com stores it with the time it arrived. No name, no email, no content; it rides on a check A9 Voice already makes. See Anonymous usage counts.
  • The sidebar starts open. On a new install it is open at any window width, where before it shrank to icons below 1150 pixels. Shrink sidebar at the bottom still shrinks it, and A9 Voice remembers your choice.

0.7.14 October 13, 2026

  • The Live window holds still while you name someone. The transcript no longer rides up under your pointer: it pauses while the pointer is over it (Live · paused) and while a name box is open (Live · paused while naming), then jumps to live. The name box opens once, in one size everywhere, and only its list of people scrolls, so Name and Not sure are never cut off, in a short window too.
  • Say yes or no to a guess in one click. A guess like Kim Jansen? shows ✓ and ✕ on the chip. ✓ names the speaker (with Undo); ✕ opens the name box with the guess at the bottom. Clicking the chip asks Is this Kim Jansen?, and Enter means Yes.
  • Typing finds the people you saved. The list of people stopped after 40 names, so saved voices and names late in the alphabet could not be found by typing, and a guessed name offered itself as a new person. The list is no longer cut, and the same list and matching are used in the Live window, the note page and Settings › Voices. + Add now comes after the matches and says what it does: Names Speaker 4 · saves a new voice, in this meeting, or Names this line only · no voiceprint.

0.7.13 October 13, 2026

With Settings › Accessibility › Text size above 100% the dictation pill was drawn too big for its window and cut off on the right, and the recording bar and the main window were sized for the smaller text. Every window now fits what it shows, grows with the Text size and never goes off the screen; the main window's smallest size grows with it too.

0.7.1 October 11, 2026
