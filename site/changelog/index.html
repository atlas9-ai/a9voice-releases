CHANGELOG

What changed

What changed in each version of A9 Voice, newest first. New builds reach the Dev channel first and move to Stable once they have been tried; see Updates and channels.

1.0.27 October 29, 2026

  • Home shows your numbers right after the app starts or updates. The tiles used to stay empty until you opened another page and came back. They now wait for A9 Voice to finish starting, fill in on their own, and update when a dictation or a note finishes while Home is open.

1.0.26 October 27, 2026

  • A9 Voice can run from a Microsoft Store package. The program is the same one; it notices when it runs from the package. A Store copy is updated by the Store, so it makes no update checks and Settings › General › Updates just says "Updates come from the Microsoft Store.". Start with Windows uses Windows' own startup setting for apps, and a Store copy keeps its files in the package's private folder, so it does not share notes with a copy installed from a9voice.com. If both are installed, A9 Voice tells you once. Nothing changes for the version from a9voice.com. See The Microsoft Store copy.
  • Connecting Claude Desktop from a Store copy is by hand. The Store keeps a copy from changing another app's settings file, so Connectors shows the entry to paste instead of Connect. See Connector for AI apps.

1.0.25 October 27, 2026

  • A9 Voice now reopens itself if Windows closes it for a restart that doesn't finish. Run entries only start apps at sign-in, so a restart that was called off left A9 Voice closed, and a call after it was not recorded. When Windows ends the session, A9 Voice sets a one-time reminder that opens it in the tray two minutes later (the sign-in start removes it if the PC really restarts), and registers with Windows' own restart-apps path. Quitting from the tray still means quit. A recording that was running is saved and finished at the next start. See A9 Voice was closed after a restart that did not finish.
  • The log now says how A9 Voice ended and how it started: quit from the tray, Windows ending the session, a crash (with the place in the code), and on the next start the way it was started.
  • Fewer repeated audio warnings. A microphone buffer underrun is logged once with a count instead of once per callback, and it no longer makes the next dictation close and reopen the microphone.

1.0.24 October 26, 2026

  • Calls are detected again on current Windows 11. Newer Windows 11 builds stopped listing which apps use the microphone where A9 Voice looked, so Teams, Zoom, Google Meet in Chrome and the rest were never picked up and nothing started by itself. A9 Voice now also asks Windows' audio system, on every microphone and not only the default one, and a call counts when either source shows it. Start and stop timing, the Browser calls switch and its default, and the notices are unchanged.
  • Detection check (Settings › Recording): lists the apps holding each microphone right now and which of them would start a recording. Copy diagnostics and problem reports carry the same lines, and the log says which way each call was seen.

1.0.23 October 24, 2026

  • New PC? Point A9 Voice at your notes folder and it finds the notes already there. A new PC pointed at a folder that already holds your notes started with an empty list. Find notes in this folder (Settings › Notes › Storage) now offers the notes it finds, right after Change…, at startup over an empty list, or when you press Find…. It only reads the files and changes nothing until you edit a note. See Moving your notes to a new PC.
  • Export can now include your notes and learned voices. Include my notes carries summaries, transcripts, images and the details a folder can't hold, never audio. Include learned voices is off unless you tick it. Import shows what is in the file with a checkbox for each part and merges notes without replacing or deleting any.

1.0.22 October 23, 2026

  • Report a problem from Settings › General (the About card) or the tray; you see exactly what's sent. Describe what went wrong and press Send. Under Show what's sent you can read the version, Windows details, a summary of switches and engine names and the recent log lines that go with it, with keys, names and your user folder taken out. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and you get a report ID. See Reporting a problem.

1.0.21 October 23, 2026

  • Recognize people by voice stays on when you change another setting afterwards. A Settings page that was open before you turned a setting on could save its older copy and switch it back off. Pages now save only what you changed, and every page re-reads settings the app changed itself.

1.0.20 October 22, 2026

  • The Live window holds still again while you name someone. With a name box open, or the pointer over the transcript, the feed stays where it is while new lines keep arriving below. Showing the recording bar again (the tray's Show recording bar, a Stop request, the show / hide key) used to read the transcript over and jump it to the live end under your pointer; it no longer touches a feed that is held. See The Live window.

1.0.19 October 20, 2026

  • Settings › Speech services no longer shows services that aren't available yet.

1.0.18 October 20, 2026

  • Speak your own language. New installs on a non-English Windows transcribe in that language, the setting is now on the Models page as Language you speak and covers meetings as well as dictation, and summaries follow the meeting's language. If you installed earlier and Windows is in another language, a one-time card on Models asks whether to switch; nothing changes without your click. More than 60 languages are in the list, shown with their own names (German (Deutsch)), in both Language you speak and Summary language. See Models and Languages by engine.
  • A model that can't do your language says so. When the model for Dictation or Meeting notes doesn't understand the language you set (for example Parakeet V2 with German), its row turns amber with Fix this, which lists only the engines that do and recommends the multilingual model on this PC. The Dictation page shows the same line. Speech services you added yourself are never warned about.
  • Summaries and answers match the language. With Summary language on Same as the meeting, the title, headings, summary, action items and tags are written in the transcript's main language, even though the built-in section headings are in English. Ask answers in the language of your question.
  • The offline fallback no longer picks a model that can't hear your language. With no internet, dictation and notes use a model on this PC only if it understands the language you set (before, Japanese could land on Parakeet V3, which has no Japanese).

1.0.17 October 20, 2026

  • Minimize now shrinks the bar in place. The bar is one bar in three sizes (Small, Bar, and the bar with the Live feed), with the same left edge, top and screen. Minimize collapses it to Small where it is, with the animated level bars, the time and a › button, and the feed hides with it. It no longer jumps to another spot or screen, and hovering Small no longer expands it, so it can be dragged: only a click on › or on Small expands it, in place (from wherever you dragged it). Small stays above every other window, including the main A9 Voice window. The size and position are remembered across restarts, and the first spot is the lower left of your main screen. See Recording a meeting.
  • The note page says what adds screenshots. While recording it reads Use Snip (Ctrl+Alt+S) to add a screenshot to this note. and mentions Win+Shift+S only when Also add Windows screenshots to the meeting is on.

1.0.16 October 19, 2026

  • Live speaker separation now runs on your PC for free. While a meeting is going, the other people on the call get their own colors and names (Speaker 2, Speaker 3, …) in the Live window without Deepgram, AssemblyAI or any key: A9 Voice tells their voices apart on your PC, using the same speaker model as Voices, and nothing leaves your computer. It is on by itself when your live transcript is written on your PC; Settings › Notes › Live window › Speakers during the call switches it to On this PC (free) for a cloud live transcript too, or back to the service's own labels. A line appears a few seconds after the speech with its speaker, two speakers that turn out to be one person are joined, and a name is only put on a speaker when one saved voice is clearly the best match. See Speakers on this PC.
  • The note gets a final pass over the whole meeting. When the recording stops, A9 Voice reads every fingerprint again with the speakers as the meeting settled them, and the note's speakers come from that when the speech engine can't tell people apart or runs on your PC; a cloud service that labels speakers keeps its own. The names you gave during the call carry over. Each note keeps one set of numbers per speaker (no audio) in A9 Voice's data folder, never in exports, so names can be matched again later.
  • Naming a speaker in the Live window picks whoever had the floor in the last few seconds when nobody is shown as talking, and the live transcript's timing no longer drifts when the recording drops a block of audio.

1.0.15 October 19, 2026

  • Only A9 Snip adds screenshots to a meeting now. Turn on Also add Windows screenshots (Settings › Notes › Recording) to include Win+Shift+S / PrtScn. It used to take every image that reached the clipboard during a meeting, so a screenshot you pasted into a chat landed in the notes. It is off for everyone, A9 Voice reads the clipboard only for the snip you just asked for, and with it on the same picture is added once. A9 Snip has its own key, Ctrl+Alt+S to begin with, shown in the Snip button's tooltip. See Live window.
  • The Live window's Snip counter now counts. It stayed at 0 because the window read the number once, when it opened; it now follows the note as pictures are added or removed, and reads Snips this meeting (n).

1.0.14 October 19, 2026

  • Minimize can no longer lose a recording. The bar and the Live feed now go to a mini pill that is always on top and never leaves the screen: a pulsing red dot and the time (an amber pause mark when paused), lower left of the screen above the taskbar to begin with, draggable and remembered. Hover it or click it and the bar comes back where it was, with the feed as you left it. The tray click and the show / hide key switch between the bar and the pill instead of hiding everything, and A9 Voice checks the pill against Windows once a second and puts it back if it is ever hidden, minimized or off screen. See Recording a meeting.
  • Every stop asks first. The Stop in the Live window used to end the recording and start the notes at once. Now it, the sidebar and note page Stop, the tray's Stop recording and the record key all bring the bar back and ask Stop recording? with Stop, Stop & discard… and an x; nothing stops until you answer.

1.0.13 October 19, 2026

  • The Live window now docks to the recording bar; Snip and Live are always one click away. While a note records, the bar is the Live window's header: the feed hangs directly off it with no gap, lined up with the bar, and moves with it when you drag the bar or the feed's header, onto another screen too. It opens below the bar, or above it near the bottom of the screen, and never leaves the screen. Snip and a Live feed toggle (filled while the feed is open) are on the bar all the time. The chevron in the feed's lower right corner (Hide live feed) replaces the x, which could be read as ending the recording; it and the bar's button only hide the feed, and never stop or pause the recording. A9 Voice remembers whether you left the feed open, and its size, for the next meeting and the next start. See Recording a meeting and Live window.
  • Hovering the bar now only adds the microphone and computer-audio pickers. They appear to the right of the buttons, and neither the bar nor the feed under it moves.
  • The tray icon says what is happening while the bar is minimized. It shows the plain icon when idle, a red dot while recording and a pause mark while paused, and its tooltip reads A9 Voice - Recording 12:34 or A9 Voice - Paused 12:34.

1.0.12 October 16, 2026

  • Savings tells the truth about the AI model you use. The AI card prices the last 30 days' summaries and Ask on the model you use today instead of counting money spent on models you have since left, only suggests a model of the same quality tier or better (never a weaker one), and says Could save only for at least $1 a month, so cents no longer count as an idea on the tab or in the headline. A suggestion names the model (Switch to GPT-4.1 mini to save $1.40/mo) with a Switch button that changes only the AI chip. The three cards now run Dictation, Meeting notes, AI, the same names and order as the chips, and Claude Haiku 5.5's higher price for prompts over 100,000 tokens is in the price table. See Usage & cost.
  • Compare on a meeting now says what it compares against. The window names the engine that wrote the meeting you picked (Compared against: AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro, or your existing transcript for an older note that didn't keep it), and the result's left column carries the same name. The provider list used to leave out the engine in use now, which hid the very engine a meeting written on another one (a Parakeet note, after you switched to AssemblyAI) should be compared with; it now leaves out the one that wrote that meeting. See Usage & cost.

1.0.11 October 16, 2026

  • Updates no longer leave A9 Voice closed if Windows or security software interrupts them. A9 Voice used to end itself two seconds after handing over to the installer, even when Windows had not started the installer yet (it can take a while when security software looks at the new file); the installer then never ran and nothing was left open. Now A9 Voice ends only once the installer is running, a slow start only delays the update, and the installer starts the copy you already had if it cannot finish. See Updates and channels.
  • A backstop that starts A9 Voice again. Right before it hands over to the installer, A9 Voice asks Windows to start it again two minutes later if it is not running: a one-time entry in your own Task Scheduler (no administrator rights, nothing run from a temporary folder). It removes the entry the next time it starts.
  • An update that keeps failing stops after two tries in a day. A9 Voice then shows "The update didn't install. You can try again now or install it from the download page." with Try again, and tries by itself again tomorrow. Updates still never install while you are dictating or recording.
  • The installer keeps its history. installer.log in the logs folder now has a header line for every run (time, versions, how it was started) and a line for how it ended, and keeps about the last 200 KB. A9 Voice's log names what is still running if it ever has to end itself.

1.0.10 October 16, 2026

  • When a work network blocks your speech service, A9 Voice now says so and transcribes on your PC instead. A security gateway that answers for a service (a block page, a refused connection) used to show up as "Deepgram error 503", which read as a broken app. A9 Voice now tells a block from a real problem with the service: a block is said in plain words ("Your network is blocking Deepgram (api.deepgram.com). The recording is saved."), in notes, in the Live window and in every service's Test button, while a wrong key, no credit or the service being down keep their own messages.
  • A note the network blocked is still transcribed. With a speech model on this PC, the note is transcribed there by itself and says "Transcribed on this PC because your network blocks Deepgram.", with Transcribe with Deepgram for when you are on another network. Without one, the note offers Download the on-PC model and tries the cloud again by itself when your network changes, and every 15 minutes for 24 hours. After two blocked notes, Settings › Models offers once to use the on-PC engine on that network; nothing changes until you click. A9 Voice never sends your audio around a block.
  • The Live transcript moves to your PC again when the network blocks it. It does so within a few seconds and says "Live transcript on this PC (your network blocks Deepgram)." A change in 1.0.1 had stopped this from working with Deepgram. With no model on this PC the Live window says the network is blocking the service, and the recording goes on. See Troubleshooting.

1.0.9 October 15, 2026

  • Search by meaning, on your PC. Turn it on in Settings › Search › Search by meaning (a one-time download of about 24 MB, or 135 MB for a PC whose Windows is not in English) and Search, Quick and Deep answers and the connector find what you meant as well as the words you typed: ask about "funding" and the meeting that said "the budget got approved" comes up. Your notes are read once in the background at low priority (not while you record) and the numbers stay on this PC; nothing is sent anywhere. What you type word for word, like a name or a ticket number, still ranks first, and Remove goes back to words only. See Search and Ask.

1.0.8 October 15, 2026

  • The Notes list is easy to browse past a few days. Day and week headers are now clear and stay at the top while their notes scroll under them, each with how many notes it holds and how long they run (TUESDAY, OCT 13 · 7 notes · 2 h 03 m). This week is listed day by day; older notes sit under weeks that start folded (LAST WEEK · Oct 5 – 11). Click a header to fold or open it, use Fold all / Open all, and what you folded is remembered. A search or filter opens everything.
  • Everything one person said, across every meeting, with no AI. Open Everything Nick said from the Ask page's person filter, a speaker's ⋯ menu on a note, or by typing a name in Ctrl+K. It lists every line they said, newest meeting first, for a range you pick (30 days by default) and optionally containing some words, with a totals line built on your PC ("Nick spoke in 23 meetings (Sep 2 – Oct 15): 1,412 lines, about 3 h 10 m.") that also lists the meetings where their speaker isn't named yet. Copy it, or save it as .md or .csv. Ask about what Nick said is the only step that uses a model, and sends only those lines, when you press it. See Asking your notes.
  • Jump to a date. The calendar button beside the search box shows a month with a dot and a count on the days that have notes; pick one, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter, and its week opens with that day at the top. See The note page.

1.0.7 October 15, 2026

  • Ask reads a meeting whole when a question lands on three meetings or fewer. Deep used to send the same handful of passages from one meeting (6) that Quick sent (10); it now sends the summary and the full transcript, with the time and speaker on every line so citations still open the exact moment, and a meeting too long for your model's request is cut to its summary and the parts that matter most. The line under the answer says what was read ("Read the full transcript of 1 meeting (Oct 14)."). See Asking your notes.
  • A follow-up stays on the same meetings, in the same mode. It starts on the mode of the question it follows (a Deep answer's follow-up is Deep, and the control beside it can be changed) and reads only the meetings the answer cited, shown as a From the 2 meetings above chip; remove it to ask over everything.
  • Notes get tags without a click, so Ask's tag filter has tags. With AI auto-tag on, a new tag the AI suggests is added to the note (with an × to remove it) when it fits; the dashed suggestion chip is gone, and suggestions already waiting on your notes were applied once. Any tag says No tags yet until a note has one.
  • The person list in Ask reaches the last name. Anyone (and the other name pickers) stopped after 50 names, about the letter J, unless you typed.

1.0.6 October 15, 2026

  • The Meeting notes engine list now says truthfully what each engine does during the call. Under each name one chip reads Live: speakers named during the call (Deepgram, AssemblyAI), Live: delayed · speakers named during the call (a model on this PC), Live transcript · speakers after the call, or No live transcript, and the Identifies speakers badges line up in one column. The chips come from the same facts the Live window uses, so they can't drift from what it does. AssemblyAI no longer says its speakers come only after the call. See Models.

1.0.5 October 15, 2026

Each person is their own speaker to tag, Now: shows who is talking, and the names carry into the finished note, as with Deepgram. Until now each side of a call streamed to Deepgram for the whole recording at the live rate, and was then uploaded again after the call. Now it streams while the Live window is showing, plus a minute after you close it, and where it was closed the transcript says so. Your notes are made from the whole recording either way, and names you give still land on the right people. Everyone gets this new default; Settings › Notes › Live window › Live transcript has While the live window is open, Always (the old behavior) and Off. See Live window.

When another engine writes your notes, the live transcript uses a speech model on this PC if one is installed (free, and the window says so), and otherwise streams to Deepgram only if you choose Use Deepgram for the live transcript. With neither, the window says what it needs.

The new Savings tab says, for Meeting notes, Summaries & Ask and Dictation, whether you are on a good fit or could save, shows the options on a cost-versus-speed map, and can Compare on a meeting: your own audio through a second provider, side by side, with the cost shown first.
