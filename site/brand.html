Brand guidelines

A9 Voice brand guidelines

Use these when you write about, film or design for A9 Voice. A9 Voice is an Atlas9 product.

Name

Write it
A9 Voice: capital A, the numeral 9, a space, capital V.
Say it
“Ay-nine Voice.” The address is “Ay-nine voice dot com.”
Short form
A9V, only where space is tight: a tab, a file name, a key cap.
Address
a9voice.com, lowercase.
Maker
A9 Voice is an Atlas9 product.

Don’t write

  • ✕A-9 Voice
  • ✕A9voice
  • ✕A9 voice
  • ✕Scribe (an old name)
  • ✕A9 Voice app (as the name)

Logo

Three marks: the wordmark, the lockup (icon and wordmark) and the icon. Use the file made for the ground it sits on.

Dark ground is #0B0C0E. Light ground is white. The 9 is red on both; on white the light files use a deeper red (#D93A3F) so it reads as well.

Clear space

Keep a margin on every side equal to the height of the “A”. Nothing else goes inside it.

Minimum sizes

  • Wordmark: 24 px tall on screen. Show it at a height that is a multiple of 8 px (24, 32, 48, 56, 64) so its edges land on whole pixels.
  • Icon: 16 px. Below 64 px use the hand-sized icon files (16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64), not a shrunk copy.

Don’t

  • ✕ Don’t recolor the 9.

  • ✕ Don’t stretch or squash it.

  • ✕ Don’t add shadows, glows or effects.

  • ✕ Don’t rotate it.

  • ✕ Don’t put it on a busy photo without a dark scrim.

  • A9 Voice

    ✕ Don’t rebuild it in another font.

Downloads

SVG for anything that scales; transparent PNG where a tool wants a picture. Text is outlined in every SVG, so no font is needed. The PNGs are drawn from the SVG, not enlarged.

Colors

Dark first. Select a hex value to copy it.

    Page and app backgrounds.

  • Surface

    #17181Brgb(23, 24, 27)

    Cards and panels on Ground.

  • Raised

    #1E1F23rgb(30, 31, 35)

    Keys and anything lifted off Surface.

  • Line

    #2A2C31rgb(42, 44, 49)

    Borders and dividers.

  • Text

    #ECEDEErgb(236, 237, 238)

    Headlines and body copy.

  • Text 2

    #C9CBD0rgb(201, 203, 208)

    Secondary copy and long paragraphs.

  • Muted

    #9A9DA4rgb(154, 157, 164)

    Captions, hints and fine print.

  • Action blue

    #2B5BD7rgb(43, 91, 215)

    Buttons and anything clickable.

  • Signal blue

    #7AA2F7rgb(122, 162, 247)

    Highlights, accents and links on dark.

  • Record red

    #E5484Drgb(229, 72, 77)

    Only the 9 in the wordmark and real recording states.

  • Green

    #4CC38Argb(76, 195, 138)

    Success states only.

Blue is for action. Red is for recording. That’s the whole rule.

Red is never a button, heading, link, banner or price tag. The same values are in colors.json in the kit.

Type

Geist for headlines, the interface and body copy. Geist Mono for numbers, keys, code and small labels. Both are free under the SIL Open Font License. The files are in the kit, with the license. The official Geist page has the rest.

Voice and tone

  • Plain words, short sentences, verbs first. Say what it does for the person: “your notes are already done”.
  • Confident, never hype. Don’t talk about AI; talk about the result.
  • State privacy as a fact: it runs on your PC, there’s no account, nothing goes to a cloud, no bot joins your meeting.

Video and presenter

Sarah is an AI presenter. Always say so: in the video’s description or on the page, and with the platform’s AI-content label.

  • Never present her as a real employee or customer. She has no surname.
  • No lines that claim personal experience, such as “I’ve used it for years”. She says what the app does.
Look
Dusk office, city window, warm brass desk lamp. Dark sweater or white shirt. Natural, conversational, mid-shot; real-looking, not CGI.
Voice
Warm, professional, upbeat but not salesy. She says “Ay-nine Voice” and “Ay-nine voice dot com”.
Music
Light modern electronic with warm piano. Confident, no vocals, ducked under the voice.

On screen

  • Lower-thirds: Geist on a solid dark pill, white text, blue for one key word at most.
  • Captions: in the bottom third of the frame.
  • The app: only on made-up data with fictional names.
  • End card: the lockup and “Free for Windows · a9voice.com” on Ground.

Formats and safe areas

Two formats: 16:9 at 1920 × 1080 and 9:16 at 1080 × 1920. Keep logos and captions inside the frame; in the 9:16 nothing sits within 120 px of the bottom edge.

Screenshots and imagery

  • Dark UI, made-up data only: fictional people, companies and meetings.
  • Never real names, emails, logos of real companies or real recordings.
  • Photos: real-looking, warm practical light, no stock-photo clichés.
