    Usage and cost

    What your notes, dictation and Ask cost at your own keys' rates, how the estimate is made, and how to change the prices it uses.

    Last updated for A9 Voice 1.0.12

    A9 Voice is free. The speech and language services you connect bill you directly, with your own keys. Settings › Usage & cost estimates that bill. It has four tabs: Overview, Savings, History and Prices. The estimate is built from what A9 Voice sent and a price table, so your provider's own bill is always the final word.

    What is counted#

    Every time something is sent to a service you pay for, A9 Voice writes it down, with the model and how it was sent:

    • A meeting's audio, live. With the live transcript on and a Deepgram key saved, each side of the call (you, and the other people) is streamed while the Live window is open. That is billed at the provider's live rate, for each side, for as long as it streams: only the seconds that were really sent are counted, and a meeting nobody watched has none.
    • A meeting's audio, after the call. When the meeting ends, each side is uploaded again to the Meeting notes model for the final transcript, at its after-the-call rate. A side that was silent is not uploaded and costs nothing.
    • Dictation. Each clip you dictate, at the after-the-call rate of the dictation model that answered. Dictation on this PC costs nothing.
    • Summaries, Ask and Enhance. Each call to a language model, by the tokens the provider counted. OpenRouter also says what each call cost, and that number is used instead of the table.

    A meeting you watched is billed twice for the part you watched: once live, once after the call. The live part runs only while the window is open (Settings › Notes › Live window › Live transcript), and it goes to Deepgram when another engine writes the notes only if you chose that. Set it to Off if you only want the final notes.

    Models on this PC (Parakeet, Whisper) are always $0.00.

    Overview#

    Pick a period at the top right: this month (the default), last month, 90 days or All time. Everything on the tab follows it.

    • The gauge shows what you spent against an optional monthly budget. The pale arc is where the month ends if you keep this pace. With no budget the arc scales to that projection and the card says Set a budget. Over budget it turns amber and says so in words.
    • Cost per meeting hour is what a meeting costs per hour of recording, transcript and summary together. Set a target and the bar shows it, and says how many cents you are over it.
    • Meetings shows the count, the hours, and a small line of daily spend. Imported notes aren't counted: nothing was billed for them.
    • Dictation shows the cost, the count, the minutes and the engine. When dictation is free, it says about how much the same minutes would have cost on the cloud model you use for Meeting notes.
    • Summaries & Ask shows their cost, their share of everything you spend, and which providers it went to.
    • Daily spend has a bar for every day, split by provider. Point at a bar, or press the left and right arrow keys once the chart has focus, to read that day. A dashed line is the average per day.
    • By provider is the same money by provider, with one sentence about where most of it goes.
    • Most expensive lists the three notes that cost the most. Click one to open it.

    Savings#

    The Savings tab asks one question: are you on the right models for what you do? It reads the last 30 days of your own usage.

    • A verdict for each job, in the same order and with the same names as the model chips at the top: Dictation, Meeting notes and AI (summaries and Ask). Good fit means nothing comparable is both more than a quarter cheaper and at least $1 a month cheaper at your pace. Otherwise it says Could save and how much a month. Only a Could save counts toward the number on the tab and the headline. Dictation on this PC says Already free.
    • How the AI card decides. It takes the tokens your summaries and Ask used in the last 30 days and prices them on the model you use today, then on each other model you have a key for, so money you spent on a model you have since left is never counted as a saving. It only suggests a model of the same tier or better: Claude Haiku 5.5, Claude Haiku 4.5, GPT-4.1 mini, Gemini 2.5 Flash and GPT-OSS 120B are Standard; GPT-OSS 20B, GPT-4.1 nano, GPT-4o mini and Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite are Basic. A model A9 Voice has no tier for (OpenRouter's Any model, a custom API) is never suggested and never compared against, and a model with no price says so. When it says Could save it names the model (Switch to GPT-4.1 mini to save $1.40/mo) with a Switch button. A call whose prompt is over 100,000 tokens is priced at the model's long-prompt rate: Claude Haiku 5.5 charges five times its usual price for those.
    • A price check (amber) appears when a provider you use was billed in a way the price table has no rate for, or when a price you typed is far from the shipped one. It links to Prices.
    • The map puts each meeting engine by what it costs per audio hour after the call (left is cheaper) and by its live speed: Live, instant, After the call only or Live, falls behind. Your engine is ringed.
    • A card for each option: what it would cost a month at your pace, what you would save a month and a year, its live speed, how well it tells speakers apart, and its accuracy. Accuracy says Not measured yet until you run a comparison.
    • Your live transcript. Deepgram streams a live transcript from the cloud, and so does AssemblyAI when it writes your notes (billed by how long the stream stays connected). The monthly figure uses the live hours really sent, so the default (stream only while the window is open) shows up as less to pay right away. If you chose Deepgram for the live part while another engine writes the notes, a card says it stays on, billed separately.
    • A nudge for "Always". If Live transcript is set to Always and the Live window was closed for most of the streamed time in the last 30 days, a line says how many hours and what they cost, with Switch to While open to change it in one click.

    Switch on a meeting engine card changes the Meeting notes engine after you confirm, and nothing else: Dictation, Summaries and Ask keep their models, and notes you already have stay as they are. With no key saved for that provider it takes you to Settings › Models to add one. Switch on the AI card changes only the AI chip, after you confirm, to a model you already have a key for.

    Compare on a meeting sends one recent meeting's audio to a second provider after the call and shows both transcripts' word counts, how many words differ, speaker counts, the time it took and its cost. It shows the cost before it sends anything, it counts in Usage & cost like any upload, and your note is not changed. The result fills in that engine's Accuracy and Source. A model on this PC can't be compared this way. The other side of the comparison is the transcript your note already has, from whichever engine wrote that meeting, which may not be the engine you use today: the window names it above the provider list (Compared against: AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro, or your existing transcript on an older note that didn't keep it), and that engine is left out of the list, because it can't be compared with itself.

    History#

    Every note and dictation, newest first. Today and Yesterday are open. The rest of the month is one group, Earlier in October, and every past month is one line with its total. Open one and it shows 25 rows at a time, with Newer and Older buttons.

    • A day's dictations are one row, for example Dictation · 53 clips.
    • A row shows the note's title, each provider it used and how (live, after the call, on this PC), its length and its cost. Click a note to open it.
    • All, Meetings, Dictation and Ask filter the rows. The search box looks in titles.
    • Export CSV saves every row that matches the filter and the search: date, kind, title, providers, mode, units and cost.
    • The calendar colors each day by what it cost; darker is more. The box under it gives the busiest day, the longest meeting and the averages.

    A row that says Not tracked is a note nothing was recorded for. That is every note imported from another app, and any note whose engine was never written down. It is not counted as $0.00. A stretch that mixes older notes with newer ones says so: older notes were written down before live and after-the-call audio were told apart, so they are priced at the after-the-call rate.

    No usage is deleted. Clearing your dictation history or deleting a note does not take its spending out of these pages.

    Prices#

    Every estimate is units times the price of that model, in that mode.

    1. Ships with the app. Each provider's public list price, checked on the date shown on the page. A new version of A9 Voice brings new defaults.
    2. You can override any price. Got a discount or a credit? Click a price and type yours. Press Enter to keep it, Esc to cancel; clear the box to go back to the shipped number. An edited price is marked Edited and stays through every update. A price you change re-prices everything in the period, old rows too.
    3. Real cost where offered. OpenRouter reports what each call cost. A9 Voice uses that number, and its row says Real cost per call.

    Speech has two rates: After the call and Live, per audio hour. Language models have Input and Output, per 1 million tokens. A dash means that model has no rate for that mode, and anything billed that way shows no price and counts as $0.00 until you set one.

    Only the providers you use show at first. Show unused providers lists the rest. A new live rate carries a new mark the first time you open the tab. Reset to defaults removes every price you typed, after a confirmation.

    If something looks wrong#

    • The total is lower than your provider's bill. Check Live in Prices: a rate that is blank is counted as $0.00. Check for a promotion or a discount your account has; type that rate and the page follows it.
    • A note says Not tracked. It was imported, or it was recorded before A9 Voice kept usage. Nothing is hidden: it simply has no numbers.
    • Costs went up after an update. Meetings recorded before this version were priced after the call only. The live stream is now counted too, which is what Deepgram bills.
