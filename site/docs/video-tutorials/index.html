      Installing A9 Voice

      Download A9 Voice and see what your browser and Windows show for a new app that isn't code-signed yet, step by step. Then check the file's SHA-256 fingerprint on the download page if you want to be sure.

    2. 0:47

      Lesson 2 · 0:47

      Your first dictation

      Click where you want the words, hold Ctrl and Space, talk, and let go: your words are typed where your cursor is. Esc cancels, and hands-free mode is in Settings.

      Read the guide → · Getting started →

    3. 0:57

      Lesson 3 · 0:57

      Your first meeting

      Start a recording from Notes or with a key, or let it start with a call. The bar and the Live window follow along, and when it ends you get the transcript, then a summary with decisions and action items.

      Read the guide → · Live window →

    4. 0:49

      Lesson 4 · 0:49

      Naming speakers

      Turn Speaker 2 and Speaker 3 into real names: live during the call, or on the note page afterwards. Recognize people by voice is off until you turn it on; then A9 Voice names people you have named before, and the voiceprints stay on your PC.

      Read the guide → · Voices →

    5. 0:40

      Lesson 5 · 0:40

      Find anything and ask your notes

      In A9 Voice, press Ctrl + K to search your notes and dictations, or ask a question and get an answer with links to where it was said. During a call, the Live window answers questions about that meeting.

      Read the guide → · Live window →

    6. 0:40

      Lesson 6 · 0:40

      Custom words

      Teach A9 Voice your names and jargon: add words as hints, and add fixes for mistakes that keep coming back. The same lists work for dictation and for meeting notes.

      Read the guide →

    7. 0:45

      Lesson 7 · 0:45

      Your notes, your files

      Every note is a folder of plain Markdown files, with the audio kept in your Audio folder. See where they live, how Export works and how templates shape the summary.

      Read the guide → · Templates and export →

    8. 0:43

      Lesson 8 · 0:43

      Speech models and your graphics card

      Use a cloud service with your own key, or a model that runs on your PC, with the graphics card for the bigger ones. Then choose who handles dictation, who handles meeting notes and the fallback for dictation.

      Read the guide → · Models →
