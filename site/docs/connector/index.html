    Connector for AI apps (MCP)

    Let an AI app on your PC, such as Claude Desktop or ChatGPT desktop, search and read your A9 Voice notes, so you can ask it about your meetings. Claude Desktop takes it as an extension in one click. It is read only, off until you turn it on, and nothing leaves your PC except what that app reads.

    Last updated for A9 Voice 1.0.37

    The connector is a small helper that AI apps can start on your PC. Once an app is connected, you can ask it things like "What did Priya say about the approval routing?" or "What action items do I have from this week?" and it looks the answer up in your notes.

    It is also called an MCP server, which is the name the AI apps use for a connector they run on your own PC. You will see "MCP" in those apps' settings.

    What the connector does#

    • It runs on your PC. The AI app starts it itself as a small background program with no window and no tray icon. A9 Voice does not have to be open. It reads your notes from the same place A9 Voice keeps them.
    • It is read only. It can search and read. It cannot add, change or delete a note.
    • It is off by default. Until you turn it on, an app that asks gets a short message instead of your notes: "The A9 Voice connector is turned off. Turn it on in A9 Voice › Settings › Connectors."
    • You choose the apps. Nothing is shared until you connect an app yourself.

    What an AI app can ask for#

    Once you connect an app, it can use four tools. All four are marked read only.

    ToolWhat it returns
    search_notesThe best matches for some words: the note's id, title and date, the time in the meeting, who spoke, which part matched and a snippet. It can be narrowed by date range, tag, person, meeting name, or notes versus dictations. Up to 50 results (10 unless it asks for more).
    list_notesYour notes, newest first: id, title, date, length, tags and people. It can be narrowed by date, tag and person. Up to 100 (20 unless it asks for more).
    read_noteOne note: its summary (the default), its full transcript with [mm:ss] Speaker lines, or the notes you typed during the meeting.
    get_action_itemsAction items, newest first: the task, owner, due date, whether it is ticked off, and the note it came from. Done items are left out unless it asks for them.

    The tools cover your A9 Voice notes and any folders you added under Settings › Notes › Search (see Search and Ask). Your dictations are included in search results while Search dictations too in Settings › General is on. It is on for a new install; switch it off to keep dictations out.

    What an AI app can never get#

    • Audio. No recording, ever.
    • Voiceprints. The voices A9 Voice has learned (see Voices) are not part of any tool's answer.
    • Your keys and settings. The tools return notes only.

    What it can read once you connect it#

    Plainly: a connected app can read the titles, dates, summaries, full transcripts (with the speakers' names), typed notes, image captions, tags and action items of the notes it asks for, and it can ask for as many as it likes. A transcript is everything that was said in the meeting.

    When the app reads something, that text becomes part of your conversation with the app. From that point it is handled by that app and the service behind it, under their own terms and privacy settings, not by A9 Voice. A9 Voice cannot see what the app does with it afterwards. Connect only apps you trust with your meetings, and use the switch to turn the connector off when you do not want to share.

    Turn it on#

    1. Open Settings › Connectors.

    2. Switch Connector for AI apps on. The switch is marked 1 below.

    Then connect an app: Claude Desktop with one button (Add to Claude), or any other app by pasting a command. Add to Claude also turns the switch on for you.

    Add A9 Voice to Claude Desktop#

    Under Claude Desktop, click Add to Claude. A9 Voice opens its extension file, A9Voice-Connector.mcpb, and Claude Desktop brings up its own Install window.

    1. Click Add to Claude. Claude opens and asks to install A9 Voice.
    2. Choose Install in Claude.
    3. Ask Claude about a meeting. The page changes from Waiting for Claude to load it to Connected with the time Claude last started it: "Claude Desktop last loaded the connector today at 12:40 (extension)."

    If Claude was already open and does not list A9 Voice, quit it from the tray (right-click its icon, then Quit; closing the window leaves it running) and open it again.

    What the extension is#

    It is a small program that Claude starts when it needs your notes. It does nothing else: it finds A9 Voice on this PC and passes Claude's questions to it, and your notes' answers back. A9 Voice must be installed on the PC (the one from a9voice.com or the Microsoft Store), but it does not have to be open. The extension holds none of your notes and never sends anything anywhere.

    • A9 Voice never touches Claude's files on this route. Claude installs, starts and updates the extension itself, so it keeps working when Claude updates or moves its settings.
    • It works for a Store copy too. The Microsoft Store copy of A9 Voice has the same Add to Claude.
    • If A9 Voice isn't installed (you removed it, or the extension is on a PC that never had it), Claude gets one plain sentence from every request: "A9 Voice isn't installed on this PC. Get it at a9voice.com."
    • The same four tools. The extension lists exactly the tools in What an AI app can ask for, all read only.

    Save the extension file#

    Save the extension file… saves a copy of A9Voice-Connector.mcpb wherever you choose. Use it to add the extension by hand: in Claude, open Settings › Extensions and drag the file onto the page. Or send the file to the person who manages Claude for your company (next section).

    If your company manages Claude#

    On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, the owner decides which desktop extensions people can install. If Claude won't install A9 Voice, or the page keeps saying Waiting for Claude to load it after you installed it, the page says: "Your company may manage Claude's extensions. Send this file to your Claude admin." with Save the extension file….

    For the person who manages the Claude plan (an Owner or Primary Owner), in Claude's own words (Enabling and using the desktop extension allowlist):

    • You can upload custom extensions to deploy across your organization in Organization settings › Connectors › Desktop. Upload A9Voice-Connector.mcpb. It appears under Custom team extensions; choose Add to team to add it to your allowlist and turn it on for your team.
    • A custom extension you allow is scoped to your organization and can't be used by other organizations.
    • A9 Voice must be installed on each PC where someone uses it. A copy of the file is also on every release of A9 Voice (A9Voice-Connector.mcpb on the releases page).
    • Claude's pages decide the exact steps and words, and they change. If a step here does not match what you see, follow Claude's page.

    One copy only#

    If you connected through the settings file before (see Other ways to connect) and Claude now loads A9 Voice as the extension, Claude would hold it twice. The page shows one amber line: "Claude now loads A9 Voice as an extension. Remove the old settings-file entry?" with Remove.

    Remove takes the a9-voice entry out of each of Claude's settings files that has it, leaves everything else in them, and keeps a backup copy first (claude_desktop_config.json.a9v-backup beside each). Nothing is removed until you click, and the line appears only after Claude has really loaded the extension: until then the settings-file entry may be the only way Claude reaches A9 Voice. A Store copy of A9 Voice can't change Claude's files, so there the line tells you to delete the entry yourself.

    Other ways to connect#

    These keep working: a Claude Desktop that isn't the current download, other AI apps, and anything you already connected. Nothing you connected before is changed.

    Connect Claude Desktop#

    Under Other ways to connect, click Connect to Claude Desktop... (or Turn on & connect to Claude Desktop... if the switch is still off). This writes an entry into Claude's settings file instead of adding the extension.

    1. A9 Voice first shows what it will change, before it changes anything. It lists one line for each settings file it will change, with the file's path: it adds (or updates, if it is already there) an entry called a9-voice, the "A9 Voice notes connector".

    2. The rest of each file stays exactly as it is. Your other connectors and settings are not touched. If a file already exists, A9 Voice makes a backup copy of it first, in the same folder, with .a9v-backup added to the name. A file that is not valid JSON is left alone and named, and the other file still gets the entry.

    3. Click Connect (or Turn on & connect, which also turns the connector on), or Cancel to change nothing.

    4. Quit Claude from the tray and open it again. Closing Claude's window leaves it running, and it reads its settings only when it starts. Right-click Claude's icon in the tray (the arrow by the clock may be hiding it), then choose Quit.

    Which settings file Claude reads#

    There are two kinds of Claude Desktop for Windows, and they keep their settings in different places. A9 Voice finds out which you have and writes the file that one reads:

    Your Claude DesktopIts settings file
    The current download (it installs like an app from the Microsoft Store)%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\Claude_pzs8sxrjxfjjc\LocalCache\Roaming\Claude\claude_desktop_config.json
    An older install%APPDATA%\Claude\claude_desktop_config.json

    To tell which you have, look at the path under Claude Desktop on the Connectors page: it is the file Claude reads. The Open folder button beside it opens the folder. If you have both, A9 Voice writes both. Use the path on the Connectors page rather than a button inside Claude that opens its settings file: on the current Claude, that button can open a different file from the one Claude reads.

    "Connected" and "Claude loaded it"#

    This is for the settings-file way, under Other ways to connect. Connected means the A9 Voice entry is in the file Claude reads. The line under it says whether Claude has actually started the connector:

    • "Claude Desktop last loaded the connector today at 12:40." Claude started it. You can ask Claude about your meetings.

    • "Claude Desktop hasn't loaded the connector yet." Quit Claude from the tray and open it again, as above.

      If it is still not loaded after a real restart, your company may limit local connectors in Claude. Ask whoever manages your Claude plan.

    A9 Voice keeps only the name Claude gave when it started the connector, its version and the time, in a small file on your PC (connector-seen.json). It is never sent anywhere.

    If you connected before this was fixed#

    Earlier versions wrote only the older file, so on the current Claude the page said "Connected" while Claude never saw it. Now the page shows an amber message, Claude Desktop on this PC reads a different settings file, so it can't see A9 Voice yet, with a Finish connecting... button. It opens the same preview, so you see the change before it happens. A9 Voice never changes another app's file without showing you first.

    The same amber message appears when A9 Voice has moved since you connected it (the entry points to its old place). Finish connecting... updates it.

    If Claude Desktop is not installed on your PC, the page says so ("Claude Desktop isn't installed on this PC. You can add it later, or use the steps for other apps below.").

    A Microsoft Store copy#

    A Store copy of A9 Voice cannot change another app's settings file, so there is no Connect button. The page shows the file Claude reads, with Copy path, Open folder and Copy settings (JSON). Open that file (create it if it is not there), paste the settings into it (add the a9-voice entry next to any others), save, then quit Claude from the tray and open it again. The command in it is a9voice.exe in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\WindowsApps, the shortcut Windows keeps for the Store copy, and it reads the Store copy's notes. A copy from a9voice.com has its own connector and its own notes.

    Connect ChatGPT desktop or another app#

    Under Other ways to connect, Other apps gives you two things to copy.

    • Copy command copies the command that starts the connector: the full path to A9 Voice's program followed by --mcp.
    • Copy settings (JSON) copies the same thing as a settings block, for apps that read a settings file.

    The steps, as the page words them:

    • ChatGPT desktop: Settings › MCP servers › Add server. Pick STDIO, paste the command, save, then Restart. ChatGPT's web chats can't use connectors on your PC: it has to be the desktop app.
    • Cursor: paste the settings into %USERPROFILE%\.cursor\mcp.json (or Settings › MCP › Add).

    Any other app that runs local connectors (the page calls them MCP servers) can use A9 Voice the same way: paste the command, or the settings, where that app asks for a local server.

    The names and screens of those apps are theirs and can change. If a step does not match what you see, look for the app's own section on connectors or MCP servers.

    See what apps asked#

    Recent requests lists what connected apps have asked for, newest first: the time, what kind of request it was (Searched, Listed notes, Read, Action items) and what it was about, such as the words searched. It keeps the latest requests on your PC, nothing more.

    Requests made while the connector is off are not run, so they do not show here.

    Turn it off or remove it#

    • Pause sharing: switch Connector for AI apps off. Connected apps stay set up, but they get the short "turned off" message instead of your notes. Switch it back on to resume.
    • Remove the Claude extension: in Claude, open Settings › Extensions and remove A9 Voice there. A9 Voice has nothing of Claude's to clean up.
    • Remove an app that uses the settings file for good: A9 Voice has no remove button for these (the one exception is Remove in One copy only). In the other app, delete the a9-voice connector in its own settings. For Claude Desktop that means removing the a9-voice entry from claude_desktop_config.json in each of the two places listed under Which settings file Claude reads that exist on your PC (A9 Voice's backup, claude_desktop_config.json.a9v-backup beside each, is the file as it was before). Then quit Claude from the tray and open it again. The A9 Voice page shows "Connected" for as long as that entry is there.

    If something goes wrong#

    • Claude won't install the extension, or A9 Voice never appears in Claude's extensions. Your company may manage Claude's extensions. Press Save the extension file… and send the file to whoever manages your Claude plan (see If your company manages Claude). Note the exact words Claude shows: they say which.
    • Claude answers "A9 Voice isn't installed on this PC. Get it at a9voice.com." The extension is there but A9 Voice isn't on this PC. Install it from a9voice.com or the Microsoft Store, then ask again (no restart of Claude is needed).
    • A9 Voice says Connected but Claude shows nothing. Look at the line under Connected. If it says Claude hasn't loaded the connector yet, quit Claude from the tray (right-click its icon, then Quit; closing the window is not enough) and open it again. If the page shows an amber message instead, press Finish connecting...: your Claude reads a different settings file than the one A9 Voice wrote before. If it still doesn't appear after a real restart, your company may limit local connectors in Claude; ask whoever manages your Claude plan.
    • The AI app says it has no tools from A9 Voice. Quit and reopen the app fully after connecting, and check that the switch on Settings › Connectors is On.
    • The app says the connector is turned off. Switch Connector for AI apps on.
    • Connect to Claude Desktop shows an error about the file. The settings file is not valid JSON. Fix or remove it, then try again. With two settings files, the other one is still connected.
    • A9 Voice does not show Connected after you moved or reinstalled it. The connected entry points to where A9 Voice was installed. Click Finish connecting... to update it.
