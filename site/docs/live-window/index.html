    Live window

    The Live window shows the transcript as people talk, so you can name each speaker, mark moments, ask questions and add screenshots while the meeting is still going.

    Last updated for A9 Voice 1.0.10

    What it is#

    The Live window sits beside your call while a note records. It is a working copy of the transcript: each person has their own color, and you can tell A9 Voice who is who as you go. When the call ends, the finished note is made from the whole recording, and the names you gave are carried into it.

    You do not need it. A9 Voice records without it. Use it when you want names on the note, or answers and markers during the call. For starting and stopping, see Recording a meeting.

    To see it open beside a call, with the first lines coming in:

    0:57Your first meetingWatch the 1-minute video

    Top to bottom the window has:

    1. The Ask anything about this meeting… field.
    2. A header with what is recording (Teams call, or Manual recording when you started it), a level meter for Me and for Them, and the time.
    3. The speaker strip: one chip per person.
    4. The transcript. Each line has a name, the time since the recording began, and the text. A grey line is not finished yet.
    5. Now (who is talking) and the Who's talking? field.
    6. The footer: the tabs Live, Ask and Notes, the markers Action, Callout and Decision, then the microphone and computer-audio pickers, Snip, pause and stop buttons, a gear and a close button.

    The window holds still#

    The transcript follows the live end, so new lines keep pushing it up. That would move a name under your pointer just as you click it, so the window holds still when you are about to:

    • While the pointer is over the transcript the button says Live · paused. New lines keep arriving below. About a second after the pointer leaves, the window jumps to live again. Press the button to jump at once.
    • While a name box is open the button says Live · paused while naming. The box stays where it opened and the line you clicked stays where it is, whatever the feed does. When the box closes (a name, Not sure, Esc or a click outside) the window jumps to live.
    • When you scroll up to read, the button says Jump to live. Press it to catch up.

    Open it#

    By default the window opens by itself when a recording starts, whether a call started it or you did. Otherwise:

    1. Rest the mouse on the recording bar.
    2. Press the Live window button. It stays lit while the window is open.

    If the bar is minimized or hidden, a Live window button elsewhere (for example on the note page while it records) brings the bar back first, then opens the window under it.

    The window closes by itself when the recording stops. The × in the footer and the – in the header only hide it; the recording goes on, and the bar's button opens it again.

    Where it sits#

    Docked is the normal state. The window sits directly under the recording bar, lined up with the bar's fixed edge. Near the bottom of the screen it goes above the bar instead. It moves when you move the bar, hides when the bar becomes a mini pill, and comes back with the bar. Resize it from its edges; A9 Voice remembers that size.

    To put it somewhere else, drag its header (the title row). It undocks and keeps its own place and size for each screen. A button appears in the header:

    1. Press Dock under bar in the header. Or right-click the tray icon and choose Reset bar position, which also resets the bar.

    Pin on top (the pin button in the header) keeps the window above other windows. It is on to begin with. The setting is remembered.

    The smallest size is 380 by 320 pixels. At narrow sizes the markers and pickers wrap onto a second line.

    The Live transcript needs a speech source#

    The live lines come from a live speech service, separate from the transcript of the finished note. Where they come from depends on which speech model writes your notes:

    • Deepgram writes the notes. With a Deepgram key, the two sides stream to Deepgram while the window is open. Deepgram bills it per channel, at its live rate.
    • AssemblyAI writes the notes. With its key, the two sides stream to AssemblyAI's Universal-Streaming while the window is open, on the same key (no second bill). The window says Live transcript via AssemblyAI with what it costs; the other people's side has speaker labels, so each person is their own speaker to name.
    • Another cloud service writes the notes (ElevenLabs, Groq, OpenAI or one you added). If a speech model on this PC is installed and keeps up with real time, the live lines are made there, free, and the window says Live transcript on this PC (free). Nothing streams to a cloud service.
    • Another cloud service writes the notes, and no model on this PC takes the live part. Nothing streams to Deepgram on its own, because Deepgram is not the one writing your notes and would bill you separately. The window says so and offers Use Deepgram for the live transcript; once you do, it says Live transcript via Deepgram, billed separately. Without a Deepgram key it says Live transcript needs a speech model on this PC or a Deepgram key. with an Open Models link.
    • Notes use a model on this PC. The live lines are made there too, if that model keeps up. If it doesn't, the window says Live transcript needs a cloud speech key or a faster PC. The note is still recorded.

    Settings › Notes › Live window › Live transcript sets when the cloud stream runs, or turns it off. See Speech models.

    When the live transcript runs#

    The live transcript is billed by your speech provider at its live rate, and your notes are made from the whole recording after the call either way. So by default it runs while the Live window is open:

    • It starts when the window shows and keeps going for a minute after you close or minimize it, so closing and reopening quickly changes nothing.
    • When you open the window again it starts a new stream from that moment. Where the window was closed, the transcript shows one quiet line: Live transcript paused while the window was closed. Your notes will include everything.
    • Names you give while the window is open still land on the right people in the finished note. Each stream numbers its speakers on its own, so the same person can show as a second speaker after a gap; Merge puts them together.
    • The first time a gap appears, a one-time tip explains it. Got it closes it for good.

    A recording that nobody watches therefore sends no live audio at all: Usage & cost shows no live line for it. Always keeps the old behavior (the stream runs for the whole recording); Off never streams. A model on this PC isn't billed, so it keeps running whatever you choose.

    The recording itself never depends on the live transcript. The audio, Still recording?, and stopping when the call ends work the same with the stream off.

    If you hear the call from speakers instead of a headset, your microphone also hears them. A9 Voice does not show those repeats as lines from you.

    Name the people#

    If a live source can't tell the other people apart (the window then says This service can't tell the other speakers apart live. Your notes will.), a name you give covers the line being spoken and the speech that follows it until a pause, not everything that side has said so far. Undo takes it back, and the finished note separates them.

    The speaker strip and Now#

    The strip lists people in the order they first spoke, except that you come first once you have said something, under your own name from Settings › Account. Everyone else starts as Speaker 2, Speaker 3 and so on. Each has a color; the first seven are different, then the colors repeat.

    • The chip of whoever is speaking glows, and stays lit for a few seconds after they stop.
    • Now shows only who is speaking now, for example Now: Speaker 2, or Now: — before anyone has spoken. Under it, small and grey, after Marcus says who spoke before them. A new speaker only shows after about 1.5 seconds of continuous speech, so a short "yes" does not make it flicker.
    • Your own chip is not clickable. It is you.

    With many people on the call the strip wraps and scrolls:

    Name a speaker from their chip#

    1. Click the chip of the person you want to name.
    2. The box asks Who is Speaker 2? (or Rename Speaker 2 if they already have a name). Type a name, or pick one from the list. The list is one list of everyone you might mean: names you used in this call first, then the people already on the call, your saved voices (if you use Voices) and names from your other notes. Typing narrows it down by first name, last name or the start of either, in any case: ril and thom both find Riley Thompson, and K puts Kim Jansen above Ana Sharkey. A name already used on this call shows whose it is, and the speaker's own name is listed too, marked current. A name that is not on the list shows last, as + Add "name" to this meeting. Use ↑ and ↓ to move, Enter to choose and Esc to cancel. The note page uses this same picker when you name speakers there.
    3. Press Enter or Name. The name goes on every line from that person, past and future, and into the finished note.
    4. Press Not sure to close the box without naming anyone.

    The box is the same size wherever it opens, and it never covers its own buttons. In a short window only the list of people shrinks (and scrolls); the title, the field, Name, Not sure and Apply to stay in view.

    A9 Voice will not let you give a speaker your own name: That's your own name. Pick the person you mean.

    What "+ Add" does#

    The line under + Add says exactly what pressing it will do:

    • Names Speaker 2 · saves a new voice: everything from this speaker gets the name, and with Voices on, the name can teach a new voice after the call.
    • Names Speaker 2 in this meeting: the same, with Voices off. Only this note is named.
    • Names this line only · no voiceprint: one line, with Just this line chosen. A single line never teaches a voice.

    Add someone who hasn't spoken: + Person#

    Sometimes you know who is on the call before they have said a word, or one of the people on a track is somebody else. + Person at the end of the strip adds a person to this meeting without waiting for their voice.

    1. Press + Person. The box asks Add someone to this meeting.
    2. Type a name, or pick one from the list (your saved voices and names from other notes are in it, and typing narrows it down as everywhere else). People already on the call are not listed again.
    3. Press Enter or Add. A pill with a dashed outline joins the strip. Rest the pointer on it and its tooltip says no voice yet.

    An added person has no track and no voiceprint, and adding them changes no line. They are a name you can pick from any line's menu (next section). The × next to the pill takes it away; lines you already named for them keep the name. When someone on the call later gets the same name, the pill goes and that person's chip is used.

    Answer a guess#

    With Voices on, A9 Voice can guess who is talking from their voice. A guess shows on the chip as Kim Jansen?.

    1. Rest the pointer on the chip. A ✓ and a ✕ appear next to it.
    2. Press ✓ if it is right. It names the whole speaker, as if you had typed the name, and shows the Undo line.
    3. Press ✕ if it is not them. The chip goes back to Speaker 4 and the name box opens with the guess at the bottom of the list, so you can pick the right person.

    Or click the chip itself. The box asks Is this Kim Jansen? with ✓ Yes and Someone else…. Enter means Yes. Someone else… takes you to the field.

    In a line's name box (next section), the first row is ✓ Yes, Kim Jansen when the line's speaker is a guess.

    Name whoever is talking: Who's talking?#

    Use this when someone is talking right now and you want to name them without hunting for their chip.

    1. Click the Who's talking? Type a name field and start typing a name. On the first letter, A9 Voice locks on to the person the window shows as talking (not you), or if nobody is, the last other person who spoke. The line above the field changes to Naming Speaker 2 (locked).

    2. Press Enter (or Tag) to apply the name. Press Esc to cancel.

    3. If it locked on the wrong person, press change and choose who you meant.

    After a name is applied, the bar says Tagged Ana ✓ for a few seconds (the × closes it sooner; in a very short window it stays until the next change). The field goes back to Who's talking? Type a name.

    If nobody else has spoken yet, it says Nobody else has talked yet. Click a speaker's chip to name them.

    Fix one line, or everything from a speaker#

    Sometimes the person is right but one line is wrong, or a speaker is really two people.

    1. Click the name above a line. Lines that are not finished are greyed and cannot be clicked yet.
    2. The box asks This is →. Do one of:
      • Type a name, then press Enter or Name.
      • Pick a person from People.
      • Pick one of the pills under In this meeting, or press its number. The pills are the people on the call, then the people you added with + Person, numbered 1 to 9. With the field empty, pressing a number does what clicking that pill does: a person on the call takes the line; an added person's name goes on it. Once you have typed a letter, digits are part of the name.
      • If the line's speaker is a guess, press the first row, ✓ Yes, Kim Jansen.
    3. Under Apply to, choose Just this line or Everything from that speaker. A speaker who has no name yet starts on Everything from, since you most likely mean "this is Ana". A speaker who already has a name starts on Just this line, since you are correcting one mistake.

    • A line named on its own shows its name with a dotted underline. Click it again to correct it.
    • Typing the name of someone already on the call moves the line to that person. Typing your own name moves it to you.
    • This is the quick way to say "this isn't Ana, it's Riley": click the name above the line, press the number of Riley's pill, done. Apply to decides whether it is that line or everything from the speaker, as it does for a click.
    • Everything from you cannot be renamed or turned into someone else; fix those lines one at a time.
    • With Everything from and a speaker chosen, the two speakers are joined into one (see the next section).

    One name, one person#

    A name can belong to only one speaker. If you give a name that someone on the call already has, A9 Voice asks instead of deciding:

    1. Read Marcus is already Speaker 3. Same person?
    2. Press Merge if it is the same person. This speaker's lines, and everything they say from now on, join the other speaker, who keeps their name.
    3. Press No, different person if two people share a name. This one becomes Marcus (2). A name made this way is never used to learn a voice.
    4. Press Esc to close the question and do nothing.

    Undo#

    Every naming action, including a merge, shows a line with what changed and an Undo button for 10 seconds.

    1. Press Undo within 10 seconds. The window says Undone.
    2. Only the newest action can be undone, and the button goes after 10 seconds. An older action cannot be undone any more (Too late to undo that one.). Rename the person again to change it.

    What is learned from your names#

    • Nothing is learned during the call. Undo leaves no trace.
    • A name you gave to a whole speaker can teach that person's voice after the call, but only if Voices is on. A name on a single line, and a name made different like Marcus (2), never teaches a voice.
    • With Voices on and people already saved, A9 Voice may name someone by itself after they have spoken for about 8 seconds. A sure match names them everywhere. A likely match shows Priya? with a Yes button next to their lines. Pressing Yes counts as a name you gave. Nothing is learned during the call from this either.
    • Your names are carried into the finished note by who was talking at the moment you named them. Look at the speakers on the note page when it is ready: The note page.

    Ask during the meeting#

    To see a question asked in the Live window, and the same in your notes:

    0:40Find anything and ask your notesWatch the 40-second video

    1. Type a question in the field at the top, for example What was the problem with the approval routing?, and press Enter.
    2. The Ask tab shows your question and the answer. It is written from the transcript so far, using the names you gave, and cites the moments as time chips. Click a chip to jump to that line.
    3. Pick the language model in the list under the answer. With none set up, the tab says Add a language model key in Settings › Models to ask. See Language models.

    If nothing has been transcribed yet, Ask says so. The transcript text so far goes to the language model you chose, like any Ask. To ask across your finished notes, use the Ask page: see Asking your notes.

    Mark moments and add notes#

    The Notes tab holds what you add during the call. Each item lands in the note at that moment.

    1. Press Action, Callout or Decision in the footer. A9 Voice stamps this moment and keeps what was just said (the latest line, with its speaker). The header says, for example, Decision marked at 12:26.
    2. To write your own, open the Notes tab, type in Type a note; Enter adds it at this moment, and press Enter.

    They show up in the note at that time. See The note page.

    Add a screenshot#

    1. Press Snip in the footer (the number beside it counts the pictures so far) or on the recording bar, or press Win+Shift+S. The Windows snipping tool opens.
    2. Choose the area. A9 Voice saves it to the note, and the bar shows a thumbnail with Saved to note at 12:03.
    3. Press Undo on that card if you took the wrong one.

    Any picture you copy to the clipboard while the recording runs is saved too, from any snipping tool. A picture copied before the recording started is not. You can also set a Snip key in Settings › Notes › Recording.

    Snips are placed in the transcript at the moment you took them and are kept in the note's folder under images. Snip works only while a recording is running.

    Other buttons#

    • Open the note (the document icon in the header) opens the note in the main window.
    • The microphone and speaker buttons choose what is recorded, like the bar. Same as Transcribe / default (microphone) and Windows default (computer audio) follow the usual choice; Off stops that side.
    • The pause and stop buttons work like the bar's. The stop button here stops and keeps the recording without asking.
    • The gear opens Settings › Notes › Live window.

    Settings for the Live window#

    • Live transcript: when the cloud stream runs. While the live window is open (the default) streams only while the window is showing, plus a minute. Always streams the whole recording. Off means no Live window text; the note is still made afterwards.
    • Use Deepgram for the live transcript: shown when another engine writes your notes. Off by default; Deepgram bills the stream on top of that engine.
    • Open the Live window when a meeting starts: it opens docked under the recording bar and moves with it. Off means you open it from the bar's Live window button.
    • Keep dictation out of notes: what you dictate into other apps during a meeting is not recorded as part of it.

    If something goes wrong#

    • The window says Listening… and stays empty. Nobody has spoken on either side yet, or Computer audio is Off. Check the bar's pickers.
    • The window says "Your network is blocking Deepgram". The network answered in the service's place (a block page), so the Live connection can't be made. The recording goes on and the full transcript with speakers is made when you stop. With a speech model installed on this PC the Live transcript switches to it within a few seconds and says Live transcript on this PC (your network blocks Deepgram); nothing is sent to a second paid service. See Troubleshooting.
    • The window says "Live transcript can't connect from this network". The connection to the speech service is blocked, often on a work network. The recording goes on and the full transcript with speakers is made when you stop. A9 Voice keeps trying every 30 seconds, and the line goes away when it connects. With a speech model installed on this PC it switches to that and says "Live transcript on this PC".
    • My words show up under someone else, or theirs under me. Click the name above the line and move it. A headset prevents most of this.
    • A name will not stick. It may belong to another speaker; look for the Same person? question.
    • The window is gone. Press Live window on the bar. If the bar is gone, press Ctrl+Alt+B.
