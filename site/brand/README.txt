A9 VOICE BRAND KIT
Public guidelines: https://a9voice.com/brand
A9 Voice is an Atlas9 product.

IN THIS KIT
  logos/svg/    Wordmark and lockup for dark and light grounds, and the icon. Text is outlined: no font needed.
  logos/png/    The same as transparent PNG. Wordmark and lockup 512, 1024 and 2000 px wide; icon 256, 512 and 1024 px square.
  fonts/        Geist and Geist Mono, Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold, as TTF and OTF. SIL Open Font License: see OFL.txt.
  colors.json   Every color by name.

NAME
  Write it A9 Voice: capital A, the numeral 9, a space, capital V.
  Say it "Ay-nine Voice". Say the address "Ay-nine voice dot com".
  Write A9V only where space is tight. The address is a9voice.com, lowercase.
  Not: A-9 Voice, A9voice, A9 voice, Scribe, "A9 Voice app" as a name.

LOGO
  Use the file made for the ground. "dark" files are for dark grounds (#0B0C0E). "light" files are for white and light grounds.
  The icon is one version; it works on both.
  Clear space: the height of the "A" on every side.
  Minimum size: the wordmark 24 px tall on screen, the icon 16 px. Show the wordmark at a height that is a multiple of 8 px.
  Do not: recolor the 9, stretch it, add shadows or effects, rotate it, put it on a busy photo without a dark scrim,
  or rebuild it in another font.

COLORS
  ground          #0B0C0E
  surface         #17181B

  green           #4CC38A
  Blue is for action. Red is for recording. That's the whole rule.
  Record red is only the 9 in the wordmark and real recording states. Never a button, heading, link, banner or price tag.

TYPE
  Geist for headlines, UI and body. Geist Mono for numbers, keys, code and small labels.
  Fallback: Segoe UI, then the system font.

VOICE
  Plain words, short sentences, verbs first. Confident, never hype. Say what it does for the person, not that it is AI.
  State privacy as fact: on your PC, no account, no cloud, no bot joins your meeting.
  Tagline: Dictation and meeting notes for Windows.

VIDEO AND PRESENTER
  Sarah is an AI presenter. Always say so: on the page, and with the platform's AI-content label.
  Never present her as a real employee or customer. No surname. No lines that claim personal experience.
  Look: dusk office, city window, warm brass desk lamp, dark sweater or white shirt. Natural, conversational, mid-shot, real-looking.
  Voice: warm, professional, upbeat but not salesy. She says "Ay-nine Voice" and "Ay-nine voice dot com".
  Lower-thirds: Geist on a solid dark pill (rgba(11,12,14,.82), 10 px radius), white text, blue for one key word at most.
  Captions in the bottom third. The app only on made-up data with fictional names.
  End card: the lockup and "Free for Windows - a9voice.com" on #0B0C0E.
  Music: light modern electronic with warm piano, confident, no vocals, ducked under the voice.
  Formats: 16:9 1920 x 1080 and 9:16 1080 x 1920. Keep logos and captions inside the safe area:
  no text within 120 px of the bottom edge of the 9:16.

IMAGERY
  Dark UI, made-up data only: fictional people, companies and meetings. No real names, emails, company logos or recordings.
  Photos: real-looking, warm practical light, no stock-photo cliches.
