A9 Voice records Teams or Zoom from your PC. Nobody else sees a bot join, and it knows who said what.

HOW IT WORKS

Install it, join your call, read your notes.

  1. Install A9 Voice. Setup takes five short steps and asks how you want your voice turned into text. It installs for your Windows user only and needs no administrator rights. Open Notes and press Try Notes free to start the 30 days: no signup, no card.
  2. Join your call as you always do. When Teams or another meeting app you picked opens your microphone, A9 Voice starts recording on its own, and you get eight seconds to discard it. Zoom is off until you turn it on in Settings › Notes › Recording. You can also start by hand with Ctrl+Alt+N.
  3. Hang up. A transcript with a name on every line, a summary and the action items appear in your Notes list, as plain files in a folder you pick.
WHAT IT DOES

It listens on your PC, not in the call.

There is no assistant to invite, admit or explain to a client.

Both sides of the call

It records your microphone and the sound your PC plays, and keeps the two apart. The meeting app keeps working as before.

Who said what

Speakers are told apart and each line carries a name. Tag a voice once, say Speaker 3 is Priya, and every line from that person carries her name. Voice matching is off until you turn it on.

Notes you own

Summaries, transcripts and pictures are plain Markdown files on your PC. Speech can be turned into text on your PC, or with your own keys.

Telling speakers apart needs a speech engine that can do it: a model on your PC with Identify speakers on, or Deepgram, ElevenLabs, AssemblyAI or Mistral. Some engines, such as Groq and OpenAI, label everyone but you as one speaker. Which models tell speakers apart.
ONE THING TO DO

Tell people you’re recording.

Because no bot joins, nothing announces the recording. Laws differ by place, and some require everyone’s consent. Telling the people on the call is up to you. More on recording consent.

Questions

Will anyone on the call see A9 Voice?+

No. It records your microphone and your computer’s audio on your PC, so nobody sees an assistant in the participant list.

Does it work with Teams and Zoom?+

Yes. It records what your PC plays and what your microphone hears, so it works with any call you can hear. It can start by itself when Teams, Zoom, Webex, Slack huddles, Discord or a call in your browser opens the microphone; Teams is on to begin with and you turn on the others in Settings › Notes › Recording.

What does it cost?+

Notes is free for 30 days, starting when you press Try Notes free, and then $49 once. Dictation is free. If you use cloud speech or language models you pay those providers directly with your own keys; models that run on your PC cost nothing per use. See pricing.

Where do my notes and recordings go?+

Into a folder you pick on your PC. With a speech model on your PC, your audio never leaves it. With a cloud service, the audio goes to the one service you chose, with your key.
