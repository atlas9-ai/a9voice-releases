BE FAIR

When Superwhisper is the better pick.

Pick Superwhisper if you work beyond a Windows PC, want to shape how your words are written, or need a team deal.

You use a Mac, iPhone or Android

Superwhisper has apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, and its Meetings feature runs on the Mac. A9 Voice runs on Windows 10 and 11 only.

You want custom writing modes

Superwhisper’s modes set tone, format and language model for each app you dictate into. A9 Voice has Enhance with AI, an opt-in cleanup, and no custom modes yet.

You transcribe recorded files

Superwhisper Pro transcribes audio and video files. A9 Voice does not transcribe a file yet.

A team needs SSO or SOC 2 paperwork

Superwhisper’s Enterprise plan has centralized billing and sign-in and model access control, and its pages say it is SOC 2 Type II certified. A9 Voice has no team admin.

THE CASE FOR A9 Voice

When A9 Voice is the better pick.

Pick A9 Voice if you work on Windows and want your calls written up by the same app that dictates.

You want meeting notes on Windows

Record Teams, Zoom or any call and get the transcript, a summary and your action items first, with speaker names. Superwhisper’s Meetings is not available on Windows.

You don’t want a bot in your calls

A9 Voice records your mic and your computer’s audio, so nothing joins the call or shows in the participant list.

You want your own keys without Pro

Use your own provider keys, or models that run on your PC, from the free tier, and pay those providers directly.

You want notes as files

Every note is a folder of Markdown on your PC, so Claude, ChatGPT or any tool that reads files can use it.

Questions

Is A9 Voice a free Superwhisper alternative for Windows?+

Yes, and Superwhisper has a free tier too: voice to text in any app with Whisper models. The difference is what sits around it. A9 Voice takes your own API keys on the free tier, where Superwhisper makes that a Pro feature, and adds meeting notes on Windows. Notes is free for 30 days and then costs $49 once.

Does Superwhisper take meeting notes on Windows?+

No. Superwhisper’s Meetings page says it is available on Mac only, and its download page says the same. On Windows it dictates. A9 Voice takes meeting notes on Windows 10 and 11 with no bot joining the call.

Does A9 Voice work on Mac?+

No. A9 Voice runs on Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Superwhisper has apps for Mac, iPhone and Android as well as Windows.

Can either one run without internet?+

Yes. Download a speech model in Settings › Speech models and A9 Voice dictates and transcribes meetings with no internet. For summaries and Ask offline, point the app at a language model running on your PC. Superwhisper says it works offline too.

Can I use A9 Voice and Superwhisper together?+

Yes, they are separate apps. Give each its own hold-to-talk key so one press doesn’t start both, and keep Superwhisper for the Mac, phone or modes you still want.
