FOUR COMPARISONS

Pick the one you use.

A9 Voice runs on Windows 10 and 11 only. Dictation is free with no word limit; meeting notes are free for 30 days and then $49 once.

A9 Voice vs Wispr Flow

Wispr Flow dictates on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android and has a bot-free Notetaker. A9 Voice has no weekly word cap on dictation and costs $49 once for Notes, against $12 to $15 a month.

A9 Voice vs Otter

Otter has a Windows desktop app that can record without a bot, plus Mac, iOS and Android apps and team workspaces. A9 Voice costs $49 once, against $8.33 to $16.99 a month.

A9 Voice vs Granola

Granola runs on Windows with no bot, and adds phone apps, integrations and team admin. A9 Voice costs $49 once, against $14 a month, and keeps notes as files on your PC.

A9 Voice vs Fireflies

Fireflies has a bot or a no-bot desktop app, team workspaces and 100+ integrations. A9 Voice names speakers with no bot, and costs $49 once, against $10 to $18 a month.
